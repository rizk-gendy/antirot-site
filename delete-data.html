Delete your Antirot data

This page explains how to delete the data of the Antirot app (by the Antirot developer on Google Play).

Steps to delete your data

  • Inside the app: open Settings, tap Start over, and confirm. This deletes your name, goals, chosen apps, habits, battery size, streaks and progress from your phone.
  • Or uninstall the app. Removing Antirot from your phone deletes everything it stored on the device.
  • Subscription records: to also delete the anonymous purchase record kept by our subscription provider, open an issue at github.com/rizk-gendy/antirot-site/issues with the title "Delete my data" (do not post personal details). We will confirm and delete it.

What is deleted

Everything Antirot stores on your device: your name, goals, chosen apps, usage time, habits, battery settings and progress. We never receive this data, so there is nothing for us to delete from our own servers.

What is kept

If you subscribed, Google Play and our subscription provider (RevenueCat) keep an anonymous purchase record (an anonymous app user ID and your subscription status) so that your purchase can be verified. Google keeps payment records under its own policies.

Retention

On request, we delete the anonymous purchase record within 30 days. Data stored on your device is deleted immediately when you use Start over or uninstall the app.
