Antirot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Antirot ("we", "the app") helps you spend less time scrolling. This policy explains what the app does with information. Antirot keeps your data on your phone. We do not run a server that stores your information, and we do not sell it.

Contact: https://github.com/rizk-gendy/antirot-site/issues

What stays on your device

  • Your name (if you give one), goals, chosen apps, habits, battery size, streaks and settings.
  • Which apps you chose to put on the battery, and how long those apps are open.
  • Anything you type in a habit, such as journal lines. These are not saved after you finish.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions and why we ask

  • Usage access (Android) / Screen Time (iOS): to measure how long the apps you chose are open, so your battery can drain. We only see time spent in the apps you pick. We never read what you do inside them.
  • Display over other apps (Android): to show Benny's lock screen on top of a chosen app when your battery is empty.
  • Foreground service notification (Android): a small ongoing notification shows while Antirot is timing your chosen apps.
  • Camera: for before and after photos in habits like "Make your bed" and for counting pushups. Camera images are processed on your phone and are not stored or sent. Photos you take for a habit are only used to complete that habit.
  • Notifications: optional reminders from Benny.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We use a subscription service provider (such as RevenueCat, if enabled) that receives an anonymous purchase identifier and your subscription status so we can unlock features. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Antirot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can revoke any permission in your phone's settings, turn off notifications, or delete the app to remove all data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
