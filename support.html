Common questions

How does the battery work?

Your battery is the screen time you give the apps you chose. It refills each morning. Habits like pushups or reading earn extra minutes on top.

An app is blocked but I still have time

Open Antirot once, then try the app again. Make sure Screen Time access is allowed for Antirot in your phone settings. On iPhone, go to Settings, Screen Time, and check that Antirot is listed.

How do I change which apps are blocked?

Open Antirot, go to Settings, and tap Add or remove apps. Your phone opens its own app picker. Antirot never sees your app list.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In Antirot, open Settings and tap Manage subscription. Or on iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Antirot. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

How do I restore my purchase?

In Antirot, open Settings and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
