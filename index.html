How it works

Three rules. Benny keeps score.

Use

Apps drain it

TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, whatever you choose. Every minute you spend takes a little charge from the same battery.

Lock

Zero means rest

At 0% Benny passes out and your chosen apps lock. Nothing is deleted. You can still call, text and use everything else.

Recharge

Habits fill it

Read, move, tidy up. Real-life habits give minutes back, and a fresh battery waits for you every morning.

Earn time

Do something good. Get minutes back.

Read a chapter, do ten pushups, make your bed. Prove it and the minutes land in today's battery.

Stays on your phone.

Antirot uses your phone's own Screen Time tools (Apple Screen Time on iPhone, Usage access on Android), so what you do in your apps stays on your device. Habit photos and journal lines are not sent anywhere. No ads, no selling data.

Questions

Good to know.

Does it delete or hide my apps?

No. They stay on your phone. When the battery hits zero they are locked until tomorrow, or until you earn minutes with a habit.

Can you see which apps I use?

No. Antirot works with your phone's own Screen Time tools and keeps everything on your device. Nothing about your apps is sent to us.

What if I cheat?

Antirot is a tool for people who want help, so you are in charge. You can turn the permissions off or delete the app any time. Emergency unlocks are limited to three a week, and habits are checked on your phone.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. Antirot is coming to both iPhone and Android.
